BITCOIN TIMELINE

Bitcoin Timeline

Every major milestone in Bitcoin's history, from the whitepaper through ETFs, record highs and the 2026 drawdown. Updated to November 2026. Each event links to the source we checked it against.

2008-08-18Genesis

bitcoin.org Domain Registered

The domain bitcoin.org was registered via anonymousspeech.com.

Source: bitcoin.org#
2008-10-31Genesis

Bitcoin Whitepaper Published

Satoshi Nakamoto published 'Bitcoin: A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System' on the cryptography mailing list.

Source: metzdowd.com#
2009-01-03Genesis

Genesis Block Mined

Block 0 mined with the message: 'The Times 03/Jan/2009 Chancellor on brink of second bailout for banks'

Source: mempool.space#
2009-01-08Genesis

Bitcoin v0.1 Released

Satoshi announced the first release of the Bitcoin software on the cryptography mailing list (19:27 UTC on 8 January).

Source: metzdowd.com#
2009-01-12Milestone

First Bitcoin Transaction

Satoshi sent 10 BTC to Hal Finney in block 170 - the first ever person-to-person Bitcoin transaction.

Source: mempool.space#
2009-02-11Satoshi

Satoshi Announces Bitcoin on P2P Foundation

Satoshi introduced Bitcoin to a wider audience on the P2P Foundation forum: 'a new open source P2P e-cash system... completely decentralized, with no central server or trusted parties.'

Source: web.archive.org#
2009-10-05Milestone

First Exchange Rate

New Liberty Standard established the first Bitcoin exchange rate: 1,309.03 BTC = $1 USD, based on electricity cost of mining.

Source: bitcoinmagazine.com#
2010-05-22Milestone

Bitcoin Pizza Day

Laszlo Hanyecz paid 10,000 BTC for two Papa John's pizzas - the first known purchase of goods with Bitcoin. Worth ~$41 at the time.

Source: en.bitcoin.it#
2010-07-11Adoption

Bitcoin v0.3 on Slashdot

Bitcoin v0.3 was mentioned on Slashdot, bringing a huge wave of new users and interest.

Source: news.slashdot.org#
2010-07-18Adoption

Mt. Gox Exchange Launches

The Mt. Gox Bitcoin exchange launched, becoming the dominant exchange for several years.

Source: bitcointalk.org#
2010-08-15Technical

Value Overflow Bug

A critical bug created 184 billion BTC in a single transaction. Satoshi quickly released a patch and the chain was forked.

Source: mempool.space#
2010-12-12Satoshi

Satoshi's Last Forum Post

Satoshi made his final post on BitcoinTalk, about denial-of-service limits. His last public message, a mailing-list note announcing version 0.3.19, followed the next day.

Source: bitcointalk.org#
2011-02-09Milestone

Bitcoin Reaches $1 USD

Bitcoin achieved parity with the US dollar for the first time.

Source: en.bitcoin.it#
2011-04-26Satoshi

Satoshi's Last Known Email

Satoshi's last known communication was an email to Gavin Andresen, asking him to stop portraying Satoshi as a mysterious, shadowy figure.

Source: web.archive.org#
2011-06-14Adoption

WikiLeaks Accepts Bitcoin

WikiLeaks began accepting Bitcoin donations after being cut off from traditional payment processors.

Source: forbes.com#
2011-06-19Technical

Mt. Gox Hacked

A compromised account let an attacker sell a large pile of coins at a nominal price, briefly crashing the Mt. Gox price from about $17 to one cent. Trading was suspended and the exchange rolled back.

Source: en.wikipedia.org#
2012-09-27Adoption

Bitcoin Foundation Launches

The Bitcoin Foundation was founded to standardise, protect and promote Bitcoin, funding core development and lobbying for the industry.

Source: en.bitcoin.it#
2012-11-28Halving

First Halving

Block reward reduced from 50 BTC to 25 BTC at block 210,000. Price was ~$12.

Source: mempool.space#
2013-03-28Milestone

Market Cap Hits $1 Billion

Bitcoin's market capitalization exceeded $1 billion for the first time.

Source: techcrunch.com#
2013-10-02Milestone

Silk Road Seized

The FBI shut down the Silk Road darknet market, a day after arresting its operator, Ross Ulbricht. Some of the seized coins were later auctioned by the US Marshals.

Source: en.wikipedia.org#
2013-11-27Milestone

BTC Hits $1,000

Bitcoin passed $1,000 for the first time on Mt. Gox.

Source: techcrunch.com#
2013-12-05Adoption

China Bans Banks From Bitcoin

China's central bank barred financial institutions from handling Bitcoin transactions, ending a Chinese-led price run and sending the price sharply lower.

Source: bloomberg.com#
2014-02-28Milestone

Mt. Gox Collapse

Mt. Gox filed for bankruptcy protection after reporting about 850,000 BTC missing (about 200,000 were later found). A pivotal moment that led to better security practices.

Source: en.wikipedia.org#
2014-03-07Satoshi

Satoshi: 'I Am Not Dorian Nakamoto'

After Newsweek claimed to have found Satoshi, Satoshi's long-silent P2P Foundation account posted 'I am not Dorian Nakamoto.' Whether Satoshi wrote it is disputed: the account may have been compromised.

Source: web.archive.org#
2015-01-26Adoption

Coinbase Launches US Exchange

Coinbase launched a US-based Bitcoin exchange approved by state regulators, billed as the first major licensed US exchange and bringing mainstream accessibility.

Source: marketplace.org#
2015-08-15Technical

Block Size Debate Erupts

Bitcoin XT, a client proposing larger blocks, was released, starting the multi-year 'block size wars' over how Bitcoin should scale.

Source: web.archive.org#
2016-07-09Halving

Second Halving

Block reward reduced from 25 BTC to 12.5 BTC at block 420,000. Price was ~$650.

Source: mempool.space#
2016-08-02Technical

Bitfinex Hack

The Bitfinex exchange lost 119,756 BTC to a hacker, one of the largest thefts in Bitcoin history. US authorities recovered a large part of the coins in February 2022.

Source: en.wikipedia.org#
2017-08-01Technical

Bitcoin Cash Fork

Bitcoin Cash (BCH) forked from Bitcoin over the block size debate. BTC maintained the original chain.

Source: en.wikipedia.org#
2017-08-24Technical

SegWit Activates

Segregated Witness activated at block 481,824 (01:57 UTC), fixing transaction malleability and enabling the Lightning Network.

Source: mempool.space#
2017-12-10Adoption

Bitcoin Futures Launch

Cboe launched Bitcoin futures, the first on a major US exchange, with CME following a week later, giving institutions a regulated way to trade Bitcoin.

Source: ir.cboe.com#
2017-12-17Milestone

BTC Hits $20,000

Bitcoin reached its then all-time high of nearly $20,000 during a massive bull run.

Source: coindesk.com#
2018-03-15Technical

Lightning Goes Live in Beta

Lightning Labs released lnd 0.4-beta, its first release for Bitcoin mainnet, after a few months of early experimental mainnet payments.

Source: lightning.engineering#
2019-06-18Adoption

Facebook Announces Libra

Facebook's Libra announcement brought global attention to cryptocurrency and spurred regulatory discussions.

Source: en.wikipedia.org#
2020-03-12Milestone

'Black Thursday' Crash

Bitcoin fell nearly 40% in a single day as markets panicked over COVID-19, one of its steepest one-day drops.

Source: bloomberg.com#
2020-05-11Halving

Third Halving

Block reward reduced from 12.5 BTC to 6.25 BTC at block 630,000. Price was ~$8,600.

Source: mempool.space#
2020-08-11Adoption

MicroStrategy Buys Bitcoin

MicroStrategy bought 21,454 BTC and became the first major public company to adopt Bitcoin as its primary treasury reserve asset.

Source: nasdaq.com#
2020-10-21Adoption

PayPal Adds Bitcoin

PayPal announced that US customers could buy, hold and sell Bitcoin, putting it in front of hundreds of millions of accounts.

Source: newsroom.paypal-corp.com#
2020-12-16Milestone

BTC Crosses $20,000

Bitcoin traded above $20,000 for the first time, a few weeks after passing its 2017 peak, opening the 2021 bull run.

Source: aljazeera.com#
2021-02-08Adoption

Tesla Buys $1.5B in Bitcoin

Tesla announced a $1.5 billion Bitcoin purchase, sending the price soaring.

Source: cnbc.com#
2021-04-14Adoption

Coinbase Lists on Nasdaq

Coinbase went public through a direct listing on Nasdaq, the first major crypto exchange to do so.

Source: fenwick.com#
2021-05-21Milestone

China Cracks Down on Mining

China's State Council signalled a crackdown on Bitcoin mining and trading. Hashrate dropped sharply as miners relocated, many to the US and Kazakhstan.

Source: euronews.com#
2021-06-09Adoption

El Salvador Legal Tender

El Salvador's congress approved a law making it the first country to adopt Bitcoin as legal tender. The law took effect on 7 September 2021.

Source: aljazeera.com#
2021-11-10Milestone

BTC Hits $69,000

Bitcoin reached a new all-time high of approximately $69,000.

Source: axios.com#
2021-11-14Technical

Taproot Upgrade Activates

The Taproot soft fork activated, bringing Schnorr signatures, MAST, and improved privacy to Bitcoin.

Source: mempool.space#
2022-05-09Milestone

Terra/Luna Collapse

The TerraUSD stablecoin lost its dollar peg and Luna collapsed, wiping out tens of billions and dragging Bitcoin below $30,000.

Source: fortune.com#
2022-11-11Milestone

FTX Files for Bankruptcy

FTX, then one of the largest exchanges, filed for bankruptcy after a run on deposits, a pivotal moment for self-custody ('not your keys, not your coins').

Source: cnbc.com#
2023-01-20Technical

Ordinals Launch

Casey Rodarmor announced that Ordinals inscriptions were ready for Bitcoin mainnet (he had made the first one on 14 December 2022), letting people inscribe images and other data onto individual satoshis, and sparking a debate about what belongs on the chain.

Source: rodarmor.com#
2023-06-15Adoption

BlackRock Files for a Bitcoin ETF

BlackRock, the world's largest asset manager, filed for a spot Bitcoin ETF, the application that eventually helped break the SEC's resistance.

Source: sec.gov#
2024-01-10Adoption

Spot Bitcoin ETFs Approved

The SEC approved 11 spot Bitcoin ETFs, opening Bitcoin to traditional investors through familiar vehicles.

Source: steptoe.com#
2024-03-14Milestone

New All-Time High Before the Halving

Bitcoin broke its 2021 record and peaked above $73,000, a month before the April halving.

Source: bloomberg.com#
2024-04-20Halving

Fourth Halving

Block reward reduced from 6.25 BTC to 3.125 BTC at block 840,000, mined at 00:09 UTC on 20 April (still 19 April in the Americas).

Source: mempool.space#
2024-12-05Milestone

BTC Hits $100,000

Bitcoin crossed the historic $100,000 mark for the first time.

Source: kitco.com#
2025-01-20Milestone

New All-Time High Near $109,000

Bitcoin set a new record around $109,000 on the day of the US presidential inauguration, on expectations of pro-crypto policy.

Source: theblock.co#
2025-01-23Adoption

First Crypto Executive Order

A US executive order on digital assets created a working group to propose a federal crypto framework, the first of several pro-crypto actions that year.

Source: federalregister.gov#
2025-03-06Adoption

US Strategic Bitcoin Reserve

Executive Order 14233 established a Strategic Bitcoin Reserve for the United States, funded by bitcoin the government has forfeited and not to be sold.

Source: federalregister.gov#
2025-05-22Milestone

Pizza Day Record: Above $111,000

On the 15th anniversary of Pizza Day, Bitcoin set a new all-time high above $111,000, making the original 10,000 BTC pizza order worth over $1.1 billion.

Source: decrypt.co#
2025-06-06Technical

Block 900,000 Mined

Block 900,000 was mined, a little over sixteen years after the genesis block.

Source: mempool.space#
2025-07-18Adoption

GENIUS Act Signed

The US signed the GENIUS Act into law, the first federal stablecoin framework, requiring reserves backing every token.

Source: whitehouse.gov#
2025-10-06Milestone

All-Time High Around $126,000

Bitcoin reached its record high of about $126,000, the peak of the cycle so far.

Source: theblock.co#
2025-10-10Technical

Bitcoin Core v30 Released

Bitcoin Core v30 raised the default OP_RETURN data limit, igniting a bitter debate over node policy and pushing more operators towards the Bitcoin Knots client.

Source: bitcoincore.org#
2025-10-10Milestone

Record $19 Billion Liquidation Day

After new US tariff threats, over $19 billion of leveraged crypto positions were liquidated in about 24 hours, the largest such event on record. Bitcoin briefly fell below $110,000.

Source: cointelegraph.com#
2025-12-31Milestone

2025 Ends Below Where It Started

Despite a record high in October, Bitcoin finished 2025 roughly 6 to 7% lower than it began (its first yearly loss since 2022), as it slid back from the peak.

Source: thestar.com.my#
2026-02-06Milestone

February 2026 Sell-Off

As about $2.6 billion of leveraged positions were liquidated in 24 hours, Bitcoin briefly fell to about $60,000 (late on 5 February, US time), more than 50% below its October record.

Source: theblock.co#
2026-04-19Technical

Bitcoin Core 31.0 Released

Bitcoin Core 31.0 was published, the first release to ship with embedded asmap data to help nodes pick peers from more diverse networks.

Source: bitcoincore.org#
2026-06-25Milestone

BTC Falls to $58,000

Bitcoin fell to about $58,000, below February's low and its lowest of 2026 to that point, amid heavy spot-ETF outflows.

Source: decrypt.co#
2026-11-21Milestone

Lowest Price Since September 2024

Bitcoin slid to roughly $55,000 intraday, its lowest level since September 2024, as rate-hike worries weighed on markets, before recovering above $60,000.

Source: investing.com#
