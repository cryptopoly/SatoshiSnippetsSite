Difficulty Epoch

Every 2,016 blocks the network retunes how hard mining is, aiming for one block every 10 minutes.

Halving Countdown

Every 210,000 blocks the new-coin reward per block is cut in half. Satoshi set this in the first release.

What am I looking at?

  • Purple blocks are a forecast: waiting transactions sorted by fee, packed into 1 MvB blocks. The highest payers go first.
  • Orange blocks are real: found by a miner, linked to the block before it by its hash, and permanent.
  • The dashed line is now. When a miner finds a block, the nearest purple block crosses it and turns orange.
  • Block times vary a lot. Ten minutes is only the average, so a 30-minute wait is normal now and then.
