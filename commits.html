SATOSHI'S COMMITS

Satoshi's Commits

Satoshi Nakamoto made 164commits to Bitcoin's original Subversion repository on SourceForge, from 2009-10-21 to 2010-12-15. Gavin Andresen began committing to the same repository in October 2010. Satoshi's last commit came three days after his last forum post of 12 December 2010, and in an April 2011 email he wrote that the project was “in good hands with Gavin and everyone.”

Source: SourceForge SVN log (author s_nakamoto), hashes from github.com/bitcoin/bitcoin, retrieved 2026-11-23. GitHub's history shows 279 commit objects credited to Satoshi because the SVN history was imported into Git more than once.
